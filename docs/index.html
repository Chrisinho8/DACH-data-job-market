What's up with the AI/data job market in the DACH region?

Every day at 08:00 UTC+2, data from job-postings across all three DACH countries gets pulled from an API (Adzuna), loaded into a Delta Lake pipeline in Databricks, and aggregated for various insights.

Which technical skills do postings ask for?

Each bar is the share of postings naming that tool, so the bars do not add to 100%. One advert can ask for Python, SQL and Airflow. These are mention rates, not requirement rates: descriptions are truncated at 500 characters, so a tool only counts if it appears early. Roles with three or fewer extracted skills are not offered in the picker.

Limitations of this tracker

  • One aggregator is not the whole market, and its coverage is not equally deep in all three countries. Cross-country comparisons should be read as indicative.
  • The posting date is the aggregator's, which may be when it indexed the job rather than when the employer published it.
  • Roles are classified from the job title with keyword rules, not from the description, which the aggregator truncates.
  • Multi-location jobs are counted more than once. The aggregator returns one city per listing and no remote flag, so a role advertised in a dozen offices becomes a dozen rows. This inflates the headline total by roughly 490 postings.
  • The AI numbers are a floor, not a total. Titles that say only "AI" or "KI" without naming a role are dropped, so the six AI families cover every posting that describes itself, not every posting that mentions AI.
  • Entry programmes are excluded (Ausbildung, duales Studium, Werkstudent, Praktikum, Trainee), along with Controlling and finance roles and data-centre infrastructure jobs pulled in by keyword search.
  • Listings link out, they are not republished. The postings browser shows title, employer, city, role and age, and every row links to the aggregator. Job descriptions and salary estimates are not redistributed.
  • A posting in the browser is not necessarily open. It was live at the last refresh (see the footer). Nothing here re-checks whether a job has since been filled or withdrawn.
